What is ChessIQ?

Train the skills that happen before your move.

ChessIQ turns mental training into short, repeatable challenges.
Instead of only solving traditional puzzles, you practise the underlying abilities that online chess demands. Sessions are quick, varied and scored so you can see where you are strong and what deserves more work.

The goal is simple: see faster, calculate cleaner, remember more and make fewer impulsive moves.
